Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Narra ("the app", "we") is a collection of interactive stories published by TeoKJ. This policy explains what information is involved when you play Narra on Android, iOS, or Steam (Windows, macOS, Linux).

In short

  • You don't need an account, and we don't run any servers of our own.
  • Your saves, settings, and reading progress stay on your device.
  • On the free mobile version, ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information and an advertising identifier.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play, the App Store, or Steam. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Narra keeps the following on your device so you can pick up where you left off: your saves, reading progress, unlocked endings, music and gallery, your settings (language, text speed, volume, screen mode), whether you have unlocked a story, and when you last watched an ad for a chapter.

This information is not sent to us. On Android, the system may include it in your Google account's device backup (Android Auto Backup), which is handled by Google under your backup settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the copy on your device.

2. Advertising (mobile version only)

In the free mobile version, some chapters show a full-screen ad before they start, unless you have unlocked that story. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device model and operating system, app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, and, where you have allowed it, show personalized ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Consent: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Narra uses Google's consent tool to ask for your choice before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the app.
  • Opting out: You can turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • No ads after unlocking: Unlocking a story removes ads for that story. The Steam version has no ads.

3. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or Steam. We only receive confirmation of which item you bought, so the app can unlock it. We do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. The privacy policy of the store you buy from applies to your payment. Restore Purchases in the app asks the store for your past purchases again.

4. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to contact the app store.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: used by Google AdMob as described in section 2.
  • Vibrate, wake lock, foreground service: used by the game engine and Google's libraries for normal operation, such as keeping the screen on while you read.
  • Storage (older Android versions only): for saving game data.

Narra does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

5. Children

Narra is not directed at children under 13. Some stories deal with death, crime, and grief. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect any ourselves. The only third parties involved are the ones described above: Google (AdMob, Google Play), Apple (App Store), and Valve (Steam).

7. Your choices and rights

Because Narra does not keep personal information on our side, there is nothing for us to access or delete. For advertising data, you can use the consent and opt-out options in section 2, or Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Your game data stays on your device. Information sent to Google by its ad library is transmitted over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. For significant changes, we will give notice in the app or on the store page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: narra.stories.support@gmail.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

叙界（Narra，以下称“本应用”或“我们”）是由 TeoKJ 发行的互动故事合集。本政策说明你在安卓、iOS 或 Steam（Windows、macOS、Linux）上使用叙界时，会涉及哪些信息。

简要说明

  • 不需要注册账号，我们也没有自己的服务器。
  • 存档、设置和阅读进度都只保存在你的设备上。
  • 手机免费版的广告由 Google AdMob 提供，它可能会收集设备信息和广告标识符。
  • 购买由 Google Play、App Store 或 Steam 处理，我们看不到你的任何支付信息。

一、保存在你设备上的信息

为了让你下次接着读，叙界会在你的设备上保存：存档、阅读进度、已解锁的结局、音乐和画册、设置（语言、文字速度、音量、画面比例）、是否已买断某个故事，以及某一章上次看广告的时间。

这些信息不会发送给我们。在安卓上，系统可能会把它们包含在你 Google 账号的设备备份里（Android 自动备份），这部分由 Google 按你的备份设置处理。卸载本应用会删除设备上的这些数据。

二、广告（仅手机版）

手机免费版中，部分章节开始前会显示一次全屏广告；已买断的故事不显示广告。广告由 Google AdMob 提供。

请求或显示广告时，Google 可能会收集并处理以下信息：设备的广告标识符、IP 地址（可据此推断大致位置）、设备型号和操作系统、应用信息，以及你与广告的互动情况。Google 用这些信息投放和统计广告、控制同一条广告的出现次数、防止欺诈，并在你允许的情况下显示个性化广告。Google 如何使用来自合作应用的信息：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • 同意设置：在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，叙界会先通过 Google 的同意工具征求你的选择，再决定是否显示个性化广告。你可以随时在应用的设置 → 广告隐私设置里修改。
  • 关闭个性化广告：你可以在设备设置里关闭个性化广告或重置广告标识符（安卓：设置 → Google → 广告，或 设置 → 隐私 → 广告；iOS：设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪）。
  • 买断后无广告：买断一个故事后，该故事不再显示广告。Steam 版没有广告。

三、购买

购买由 Google Play、Apple App Store 或 Steam 处理。我们只会收到“你买了哪一项”的确认，用来解锁对应内容；不会收到你的姓名、电子邮箱或支付信息。付款相关的隐私问题适用你所使用商店的隐私政策。应用里的恢复购买会重新向商店查询你以前的购买记录。

四、安卓权限

  • 网络和网络状态：用于加载广告、连接应用商店。
  • 广告标识符和广告服务：由 Google AdMob 使用，详见第二条。
  • 振动、保持唤醒、前台服务：由游戏引擎和 Google 组件在正常运行时使用，例如阅读时保持屏幕常亮。
  • 存储（仅较旧的安卓版本）：用于保存游戏数据。

叙界不会申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录或相册权限。

五、儿童

叙界不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，部分故事涉及死亡、犯罪和丧亲等题材。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童通过本应用提供了个人信息，请联系我们。

六、信息的共享与出售

我们不出售个人信息，也不自行收集个人信息。涉及的第三方只有上面提到的：Google（AdMob、Google Play）、Apple（App Store）和 Valve（Steam）。

七、你的选择和权利

我们这边不保存你的个人信息，因此没有需要查阅或删除的内容。广告相关的数据，你可以使用第二条里的同意和关闭选项，或 Google 的工具：https://myadcenter.google.com 。卸载本应用即可删除设备上保存的数据。如果你对 GDPR、CCPA 等法律下的权利有疑问，欢迎联系我们，我们会协助处理。

八、安全

你的游戏数据保存在你的设备上。Google 广告组件发送给 Google 的信息通过加密连接传输。

九、政策更新

本应用有变化时，我们可能会更新本政策，顶部的生效日期表示最后更新的时间。如有重大变化，我们会在应用内或商店页面上提示。

十、联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：narra.stories.support@gmail.com
